Privacy policy

diez hours, an internal tool of diez gallery. Last updated 25 September 2026.

What this tool does

diez hours shows the current opening status of diez gallery on diez.gallery and lets the gallery director mark days as open or closed. When connected to Google, it updates the special opening hours of the gallery's own Google Business Profile so that Google Maps shows the same hours as the website.

Who is responsible

diez gallery, Gibraltarstraat 74-B, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Contact: diego@diez.gallery.

Website visitors

The opening status on diez.gallery is loaded from hours.diez.gallery. This tool does not use cookies, does not track visitors and does not store any personal data about them. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may process technical data such as IP addresses in short-lived server logs, as any web server does, to deliver and secure the service.

Google account data

The tool uses Google OAuth with a single scope (business.manage) and is used only by the gallery director. It reads and updates the opening hours of the gallery's own Business Profile location, and nothing else. It does not access emails, contacts, reviews, other profiles or any other Google data.

The authorization token is stored as an encrypted environment variable on our hosting provider and is never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. Google user data is not used for advertising or any purpose other than keeping the gallery's opening hours accurate.

diez hours' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Opening hours data

Opening days, times and public notes (for example "Closed for Art Cologne") are stored in the gallery's own database and published on diez.gallery and Google Maps. This is business information, not personal data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings (Third-party apps with account access). You can contact us about this policy or your rights under the GDPR at diego@diez.gallery.
